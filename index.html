This week's spellings

No sound on iPhone? Turn off Silent mode (the switch on the side, or the bell in Control Centre) and turn the volume up. iPhones mute speech from websites in Silent mode.
Want more voices?
iPhone / iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → English (UK). Download a voice (Kate, Serena, Stephanie and Martha are women's voices). Then fully close Safari or the Home Screen app (swipe it away in the app switcher) and open it again – the voice list is only read when it starts. If the voice still isn't listed, restart the phone.
Android: Settings → search "Text-to-speech" → Google → install English (United Kingdom). Tap the ⚙️ next to it to choose which UK voice is used.
